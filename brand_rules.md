# Simple Brand Rules

1. Do not use unsupported superlatives such as "best", "cheapest", "number one", or "most powerful".
2. Do not make medical, financial, or guaranteed-result claims.
3. Keep the tone clear, positive, and useful.
4. Mention real product benefits only when they are supported by the product brief.
5. Avoid misleading urgency or fear-based language.
6. Keep ad copy concise and easy to understand.
